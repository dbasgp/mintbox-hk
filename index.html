About the studio

Started in 2017 as a Facebook page. Still small on purpose.

Annie trained in Textile Design at Central Saint Martins and spent years doing window displays for luxury brands before turning to teaching. The studio has stayed deliberately small — most sessions are one-to-one or four people max, so nobody gets left behind on the third row of stitches.

Beyond the studio, Annie has worked on installations and campaigns with IFC, Laforet, Adidas, HSBC, Ralph Lauren and Loro Piana, and runs free skill workshops with J Life Foundation for low-income families and single parents in Hong Kong.
