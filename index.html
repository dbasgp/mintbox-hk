About the studio

Started in 2017. Still small on purpose.

Annie trained in Textile Design at Central Saint Martins, then spent a few years on luxury window displays before deciding she'd rather teach. The studio caps most workshops at four people — close enough that nobody is reading over a stranger's shoulder when their first row goes wonky.

Beyond classes, Annie has built crocheted installations for IFC Mall, Laforet, Adidas, HSBC, Ralph Lauren and Loro Piana, and runs free skill workshops with J Life Foundation for low-income families and single parents in Hong Kong.
