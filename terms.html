Terms & Conditions

The short version of how we run bookings and what to expect.

Bookings

Workshop places are confirmed once payment is received. Times and dates are fixed at booking — please arrive on time, as workshops cannot be extended for late starts.

Cancellations & rescheduling

  • Reschedule for free up to 48 hours before the workshop.
  • Within 48 hours, we'll do our best to move you, but cannot guarantee a slot.
  • No-shows and same-day cancellations are non-refundable.

Materials

All materials needed for the listed project are included in the workshop fee. If you'd like to take additional yarn home or upgrade to a premium fibre, that's available at studio rates.

Children

Most workshops are suitable for ages 8 and up. Children under 12 should be accompanied by an adult unless otherwise noted on the workshop page.

Photography

We sometimes photograph finished pieces for the studio's social media. If you'd rather not appear, just let us know on the day.

Liability

The studio is a working textile space — please mind the equipment. The Mint Box Studio is not liable for personal items lost or damaged on premises.

Gift cards

Gift cards are valid for one year from purchase, transferable, and not redeemable for cash.

Questions

For anything not covered above, write to themintboxstudio@gmail.com or WhatsApp +852 6177 6949.
